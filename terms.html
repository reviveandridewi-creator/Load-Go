TERMS OF SERVICE & LIABILITY RELEASE

1. Service Scope, Booking & Deposit Policy

Quotes based on electronic, phone, or photo estimates are non-binding. Final pricing is established on-site prior to work commencement based on actual load volume, weight, physical labor required, and site accessibility.

20% Non-Refundable Deposit: A non-refundable deposit equal to 20% of the estimated job total is required at booking to lock in your service date, time slot, and crew.

Cancellation vs. Rescheduling:

  • Cancellation: If you cancel your service, the 20% deposit is strictly non-refundable and forfeited.
  • Rescheduling: If you need to reschedule, 100% of your 20% deposit will transfer directly toward a new service date. New dates are subject strictly to Load & Go LLC’s schedule and availability.

Weight & Capacity Limits: Load & Go LLC operates truck and trailer units subject to strict Gross Vehicle Weight Ratings (GVWR). We reserve the right to stop loading, adjust final pricing, or decline service if job conditions or weights exceed safe operating limits.

Customer agrees to fully indemnify and hold harmless Load & Go LLC against any fines, disposal fees, or environmental remediation costs resulting from undisclosed hazardous materials.

Customer assumes full financial responsibility for property damage—including cracked asphalt, driveway indentations, concrete surface damage, or yard tire ruts—caused by truck, trailer, or jack-stand placement when directing our crew onto soft surfaces, unpaved terrain, or non-standard driveways.

4. Item Exclusions, Electronics & Transit Coverage

Load & Go LLC assumes no liability for:

  • Contents inside customer-packed boxes, totes, or sealed bags.
  • Press-wood, particleboard, or engineered wood furniture damaged due to inherent structural fragility.
  • Internal mechanical, electrical, or software failure of appliances, TVs, or electronics after transit.
  • Weather-related exposure (rain, snow, road spray, dirt) during open or tarped trailer transit.
  • Disconnection or reconnection of water, gas, or electrical utility lines.
  • High-value items including cash, jewelry, fine art, firearms, or irreplaceable heirlooms (which customer must transport independently).
Moving Cargo Protection Notice: For moving services, standard legal liability for lost or damaged cargo is strictly capped at $0.60 per pound per article unless higher valuation coverage is selected and purchased prior to service commencement.

5. On-Site Inspection & Release of Liability

Customer (or a designated adult representative) must accompany the crew during a final post-job walk-through before the truck departs. Any claims for property damage must be documented in writing and submitted within 24 hours of service completion. To the fullest extent permitted under Wisconsin law, Customer releases Load & Go LLC, its members, and staff from liability for property damage or personal injury resulting from ordinary negligence or inherent risks associated with heavy lifting, navigating tight spaces, stairwells, and maneuvering trailer equipment.

6. Payment Terms & Remedies

Full remaining balance (after applying your 20% booking deposit) is due immediately upon completion of services via approved card, electronic payment, or cash. Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month (18% per annum) or the maximum legal rate. Customer agrees to pay all collection costs, court fees, and legal expenses incurred by Load & Go LLC to recover unpaid balances.
